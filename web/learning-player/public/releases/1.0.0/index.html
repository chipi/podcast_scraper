Release 1.0.0  ·  The first beta

A podcast player
that listens with you.

The first beta of Close Listening: every episode comes with its transcript, the points its speakers make, and who they are. Search jumps to the moment something was said, and a library keeps what you want to come back to.

iOS · Android · WebThe first buildOctober 2026

Getting in

Understand any podcast in minutes

A welcome page shows what is featured this week and how it works: search, listen, keep. Sign in with Google, or a link sent to your email, which opens the installed app. Access is limited to beta testers.

  • Three tabs: Home, Discover, Library. Search, notifications and your profile sit in the header.
  • Home starts with Continue listening, then Jump back in, your week, highlights to revisit, what's new, and recommendations.

Episode player

Insights as they are spoken

While an episode plays, its insights appear over the artwork as they are said, with who said them. The seek bar marks where the insights are.

  • Episode notes: the summary, key points, topics and people, insights by type, and a search inside the episode. Download them as Markdown or PDF.
  • The transcript follows along; mark a moment or save a line as a highlight.
  • At the end, a recap of the key points and what plays next.
  • Skip back 15, forward 30, change speed, queue, download for offline, and keep playing on the lock screen.

Topics · People · Themes · Storylines

Follow an idea across every show

Every topic, person, theme and storyline has its own page: who talks about it, how the conversation changed over time, the perspectives on it, and whether it is rising. Follow any of them.

  • Show pages say what a show is about, who is on it, and how often it updates.
  • Discover has trending shows and trends for topics, people, storylines and themes.
  • Search across every episode, with people and topics first and "Play from" on every result.

Also in 1.0.0

Everything else in the first beta

Library

  • Following, Saved, Boards, Revisit.
  • Save episodes, highlights, searches, shows and subjects, and tag them with a colour.
  • Boards collect anything, in your own order.
  • Revisit brings highlights back after two days, a week, a month and a quarter.

Offline

  • Download episodes for offline, on Wi-Fi only or also cellular, within a storage limit.
  • An "On this device" page when you are offline.

Profile and notifications

  • Profile: your account, the topics you follow, and your listening stats.
  • A notification bell in the app, push on iOS and Android, and a weekly email digest.
