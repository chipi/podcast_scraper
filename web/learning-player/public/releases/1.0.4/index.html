Release 1.0.4  ·  Beta

Shorter ways in.
Somewhere to keep going.

1.0.4 is about listening in smaller pieces without losing the thread. Hear the best minutes of a subject across many episodes, learn one a day for a week, or play an episode's strongest moments before deciding to stay. And the app now remembers where you were in all of it.

New

Short listens

Pick a topic, theme or storyline and hear its best minutes from many episodes, one clip after another. The page tells you up front what you are about to hear: how long, how many clips, from how many episodes.

  • Start one from the ▶ N min on this button on any topic, theme or storyline, or from the Short listens row in Discover.
  • One bar for the whole listen, ‹ and › to move between clips, and an Up next list with each episode's cover. Tap any clip to jump to it.
  • Clips stop before ad breaks and where the speaker stops, and a short pause between clips keeps it from sounding jumpy.
  • Liked a clip? Listen to the full episode offers Play on from 12:34 or Play from the start.
  • Leave half-way and Home's In progress resumes you at the clip you stopped on.

New

Learn this in 7 days

A gentler pace: one episode clip a day on a theme or storyline, for a week. Today's day is open; earlier days fold away and later ones wait their turn.

  • Start from Learn this in 7 days on a theme or storyline. You can learn up to three things at once.
  • Hearing the day's clip marks it done. Finished early? Next day now opens tomorrow's.
  • Play all plays every day you have not heard yet, back to back.
  • A storyline's path opens with a summary of the whole thread.
  • Finished paths are listed in Profile › Stats under Paths you finished.

New

Moments

Not sure an episode is for you? Play its strongest points, one clip each, right inside the artwork: who is speaking, where you are, and the next one a tap away.

  • Open it from Play moments at the top of any episode's ⋯ menu, from the Brief, or from the Moments door on the side of the artwork.
  • The list under the player ticks each moment you have heard, now or on an earlier visit.
  • When the moments end, the next episode in your queue offers its own.
  • Downloaded episodes play their moments offline.

Home

Everything you started, in one place

A single In progress row under Continue listening holds your Learn paths, the short listen you left half-way, and every episode you started. "Jump back in" is folded into it.

  • Mark as played, where episodes wait: ✓ Played sits next to ▶ Resume, and Mark as played is in the ⋯ menu of In progress and Up next cards.
  • A finished Learn path leaves In progress at once.
  • The week prompt counts moments and learning days too, and shows minutes when it has been under an hour.

Discover · New tab: Explore

What to hear next, and a place to look around

Discover is now about what to hear next: quote cards that say why they are there (Rising: …, Because you highlighted …) and play from the exact line. Browsing moved to a new Explore tab.

  • Under Mine, rails follow your own listening: More from what you captured, Continue the thread, People you keep hearing, Hear another view.
  • Less like this on any card, with Undo, and a list to reverse it later in Profile › Interests › Suggested less.
  • Explore holds search, Trends and the Episodes and Shows lists with all their filters. On a phone the list search folds into a 🔍 to keep the filters on one row.
  • Discover stops offering short listens you have mostly heard and paths you have finished.

Search

Subjects first, then one card per episode

People, topics, themes and storylines that match your words come first, as coloured pills. Below them, each episode is one card with what matched inside it, grouped: titles and summaries, insights, then the conversation.

  • Your words are marked in every passage, and insights show their type.
  • A bookmark on any passage saves it as a highlight.
  • Show more inside a card, and Show more episodes below the list.
  • Your boards show up in results, and a common word like "risk" now finds episodes.
  • Recent searches belong to your account and follow you across devices.

Episode player

The Brief, and a search that finds what you remember

"Episode notes" is now the Brief. Its search finds the line you half remember: word for word first, then other forms of the word ("invested" finds "investing"), across the whole episode.

  • Results: Insights first, then In the conversation, three each with Show more, each with Play from and a bookmark.
  • On the player, a band down the artwork holds three doors: Moments, Brief, About (the publisher's full description, with links you can tap).
  • Step between insights with the arrows, a swipe, or [ ] on a keyboard; Jumped to 1:24 tells you where you went.
  • Pull a sheet's handle down to close it. A person or topic opened from the Brief opens over it, and closing returns you there.

Library › Collections

Boards that brief you back

Library's boards tab is now Collections, with Boards and Notes each in their own section. Open a board and tap Brief: what it holds, its own short listen, its top voices and key points.

  • Once a board holds two or more episodes, a short summary of what it is about appears in its Brief, a few minutes after you stop changing it.
  • + New board opens the name field; boards can be filtered, sorted and shown as a list or a grid.
  • Notes get their own filter, a sort (newest, oldest, A–Z) and one chip for the kind of note.

Topics · Themes · Storylines

Subjects that tell you where they are going

Every topic, theme and storyline page now starts the same way: the kind in its colour, the title, Follow, and the two ways to listen. Storylines open with a summary of the whole thread.

  • A theme's topics list its lead first, marked, then what is new and growing, steady, fading, and gone.
  • A storyline shows its old names (Also called …) and says when it has gone quiet.
  • Storylines keep who they are across nightly updates, so what you follow, save or learn stays attached.
  • Something you saved that no longer exists says No longer available, with a way to remove it.

Shows in other languages · Licences

Translated quotes say so. Shows say what you may do.

Quotes from episodes in another language are labelled Translated from Spanish (or Italian, French, German, Portuguese), and the label stays when you save, share or export them.

  • A Language filter on Episodes and Shows, and a language badge on each, when the library holds more than one language.
  • A show's page carries a Licence and copyright line that opens what its feed declares: the notice, the licence and its conditions.
  • New episodes in every language get cleaner transcripts: invented sign-off lines removed, missed speech recovered, punctuation repaired.

Also in 1.0.4

Smaller things you may notice

Player & playback

  • The mini-player is for playback only, and names what is playing: Moments · 2 / 6, Learn · day 3, or a short listen's subject.
  • Home's Resume no longer opens the player at 0:00 while the audio loads.
  • Leaving Moments no longer shows a false "Couldn't load the audio".
  • Closing a sheet no longer leaves a yellow ring on the button that opened it.

Lists & cards

  • Tap anywhere on an episode card to open the episode, paused.
  • A Played badge on search results, Saved and Revisit.
  • Episodes: a compact Which episodes chip (All shows / Following / Mine) and a working In progress filter.
  • Show pages group what they are about by kind, and star what is distinctive to the show.

Profile & Stats

  • Stats tells one story: time listened, moments, learning days and episodes, a day chart, streaks, and how often a moment led you to the full episode.
  • Week, Month and This year switch with one control.
  • Synced settings now save from the iOS and Android apps too iOS · Android

Notifications & email

  • Tapping a push opens what it is about, not just the app iOS · Android
  • A "new episodes" push about several episodes opens Home's What's new.
  • Your digest email labels translated quotes too.

Dictation

  • Tapping the mic to stop keeps your last word Android
  • The mic switches off when Android's recogniser stops, and your episode resumes Android

Touch

  • No more highlight border left on a card after you touch or drag it iOS
  • Search, filters and toolbars fit on one row on small phones.
