Release 1.0.2  ·  Beta

A quick fix-up.

A small release, out the same day as 1.0.1: the app can now tell you when an update is out, Sign in with Apple can appear, rows in What's new and Saved are tidier, and push notifications stay on.

iOS · Android · WebFollows 1.0.1October 2026

Updates

  • "Update available" can now appear: "Version … is out. Update to get the latest." Earlier builds could never show it, so 1.0.2 is the first that tells you about the next version iOS · Android

Sign-in

  • Sign in with Apple can appear next to Google, once it is switched on for everyone.

Home and Library

  • In What's new, rows after the first carry only queue and ⋯; Save moved into the ⋯ menu.
  • In Saved, an episode's heart removes it in one tap, and Add to queue moved into the ⋯ menu.
  • A show saved with a colour gets the same coloured bar as an episode.

Notifications and links

  • Push notifications no longer go quiet after your phone renews its registration iOS · Android
  • A shared topic link shows a preview in messaging apps.
  • closelistening.app links open the installed app Android

Look

  • The orange waveform replaces the old "L" placeholder as the icon of the web app on your home screen.
  • 129 episodes that had no transcript button now have one.
