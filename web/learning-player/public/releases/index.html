Known issues

What we know is not right yet

We are working on each of these. You do not need to report them, but tell us if one gets in your way.

Updated 15 October 2026

Who is speaking

  • Some speakers have the wrong role or the wrong name.A guest can be shown as the host, or the other way round, and a name taken from an episode title can be misspelled.
  • Ad and promo voices sometimes appear as guests.A sponsor read or a promo for another show can be listed among an episode's people.
  • Some episodes do not name their speakers yet.Hosts who never introduce themselves can go unnamed, and some insights and moments wait until a speaker is named.
  • Similar names can get mixed up in other languages.On shows in Spanish, Italian, French, German or Portuguese, two people with close names can be merged, or a first name swapped.

Clips and ads

  • On a few shows a clip can play the wrong moment.Some podcasts change the ads inside an episode over time, which shifts everything after them.
  • Some episode openings have no insights or clips.A sponsor read early in an episode can take the opening minutes with it.
  • A sponsor read can still appear in a transcript.Mostly in the middle of an episode, or in a translated episode's English text.

Transcripts and subjects

  • A few older transcripts have little punctuation.Newer episodes are not affected.
  • A transcript's last lines can be wrong.They can repeat, or include words that were not said.
  • Many episodes are not part of a storyline yet.And "Conversation over time" is empty for most topics.
