Release 1.0.1  ·  Beta

Your account, your interests,
one colour per kind.

1.0.1 added the account and privacy controls a store app needs, a proper Interests tab, a colour for each kind of subject, and plainer words for saving, sharing and playing.

iOS · Android · WebFollows 1.0.0October 2026

Profile › Account

Your data, in your hands

Delete account explains what goes and what stays, and only runs once you type DELETE. Clear listening history removes positions, played marks and listening time, and keeps your library.

  • A real privacy policy, a first version of the terms of use, and the list of third-party software, from Settings.
  • Sign-in now says that continuing means agreeing to the terms and the privacy policy.
  • Choosing Take Photo for your profile picture no longer closes the app iOS
  • If your phone closes the app in the background, reopening it returns you to the same screen, with your episode paused where you left it iOS · Android

Profile › Interests

Interests you can actually manage

The Topics tab became Interests, with one section each for topics, people, themes and storylines. + Add searches everything of that kind, with suggestions, and changes save at once.

  • Each kind has its own colour everywhere: topics violet, people orange, themes blue, storylines cyan.
  • A subject's momentum says ↓ Cooling or → Steady when that is true, not always "Rising".
  • Long lists show Show more (N), and sections fold.

Also in 1.0.1

Smaller things you may notice

Save, boards, share

  • The heart reads Save; "Add to collection" is Add to board.
  • Share cards are pictures made for you, with new cards for themes and highlighted quotes.
  • "Share link" and "Share text" are Copy link and Copy text.

Player and notes

  • One ▶ Play from everywhere, and every one starts playback.
  • One Download notes opens a viewer with Markdown and Print or share.
  • Episodes in other languages have a Transcript switch between the original and English.
  • Lock-screen and notification controls, and the audio output switcher Android

Pages and lists

  • One page width everywhere, and sheets stop above the phone's bottom bar.
  • A show's chart reads "Episodes per month"; tap a bar to jump to that month.
  • Search finds shows too.
  • Episode cards show the publisher's description.

Reliability

  • After an update, the app no longer runs the previous version's code on its first launch iOS · Android
  • A follow made just before a reload no longer shows "+ Follow".
  • Back, and closing a sheet, return you to where you were.
