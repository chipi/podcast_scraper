Release 1.0.3  ·  Beta

A Home that starts
from you.

1.0.3 rebuilt Home around the listener: a guided start for a new account, recommendations from what you actually listen to, and your own trends first. Episode notes got shorter and easier to read, and Android stopped drawing blank areas.

iOS · Android · WebFollows 1.0.2October 2026

Home

A guided start, then a Home that is yours

A new account is welcomed by name and walked through three short steps: pick at least three topics, people, themes or storylines, follow a few shows, and you are set.

  • Skip step skips one step; Not now hides the guide for three days. Settings can show it again.
  • Recommended for you moved up, and says what it is picked from: what you listen to, or what you follow.
  • What's new is about your own shows and topics, and falls back to everything new when you follow nothing yet.
  • "Your Week" became Your week in review: what you listened to, what you saved, and what is rising in what you heard. It stays hidden while there is nothing to show.
  • Trends moved off Home to Discover. The strip at the foot of Home now reads Explore what people are talking about.

Discover

Your trends, first

Trends now open on your own listening: what is rising in the topics, themes, storylines and people you hear. Switch to everyone's trends, or from rising to most talked about, with one tap.

  • Until you have listened enough, it says Your trends fill in as you listen, save and follow and offers everyone's.
  • Your trends refresh when you come back to Discover.

Episode notes

Notes you can read at a glance

Episode notes open short: two key points, one row of topics and people, four insights, three related episodes. Show more opens the rest.

  • A line explains what insights are, and What do these types mean? opens a legend for claims, observations, recommendations and questions.
  • Play from sits at the end of each perspective, where you finish reading it.
  • The "Insight density" chart is gone.
  • Android: parts of the notes and "More like this" no longer turn black Android

Boards

Save to a board in one sheet

Saving to a board opens one sheet listing every board with its picture and count: ✓ where the episode already is, ⊕ to add it, and + New board. "Collections" are called boards everywhere now.

  • A new board shows in Library at once.
  • Revisit counts, your week, Recently played and Home's boards update after a change made elsewhere.
  • Library's "Find …" boxes are now Filter ….

Also in 1.0.3

Smaller things you may notice

Player

  • The mini-player has a ✕ that stops playback and keeps your place.
  • After saving a moment, its bookmark stays a round icon instead of a pill that slid off screen.
  • No more blur behind the controls over the artwork.

Lists and speed

  • Topic, person, theme and storyline pages show five episodes and three voices at first, with Show more.
  • Saved, highlights, notes and board items load a page at a time.
  • Search results no longer wait for the people-and-topics lookup.
  • Your week and the bell load faster.

Sign-in and sharing

  • One Sign in button: "New here? Your first sign-in creates your account."
  • Switching accounts never shows the previous account's things.
  • One share icon everywhere, and closing the share sheet no longer shows an error iOS · Android

Settings and more

  • Queue cards show episode descriptions again.
  • The bell refreshes when you come back to the app.
  • New in Settings: Copy debug info and Show the getting-started guide again.
  • Emails show an episode's whole summary.
